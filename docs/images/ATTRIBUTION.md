# Image attribution

The two peppered moth photos in this folder come from Wikimedia Commons. They are the two morph photos shown in the "Evolution" section of the English Wikipedia article [Peppered moth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peppered_moth).

| Local file | Commons file | Morph | Author | License |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `peppered-moth-typica.jpg` | [File:Biston.betularia.7200.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Biston.betularia.7200.jpg) | *typica* (pale) | Olaf Leillinger (Commons user Olei), cropped by Chiswick Chap | [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/) (also CC BY-SA 2.5 and GFDL) |
| `peppered-moth-carbonaria.jpg` | [File:Biston.betularia.f.carbonaria.7209.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Biston.betularia.f.carbonaria.7209.jpg) | *carbonaria* (melanic) | Olaf Leillinger (Commons user Olei), cropped by Chiswick Chap | [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/) (also CC BY-SA 2.5 and GFDL) |

- **Originals:** typica is 2798×1662 px, taken 2006-06-14. Carbonaria is 2816×1366 px, taken 2006-06-13 (2006-06-19 according to its EXIF data). Both were photographed in Dresden, Saxony, Germany.
- **Authorship note:** The Commons "Author" field (and the API's `Artist`) lists Chiswick Chap. That name was filled in by an automated `{{Information}}` edit in 2018. The original 2006 uploads by Olei state "picture taken by Olaf Leillinger". Chiswick Chap only cropped the images, in 2017. These credits name both.
- **Changes made here:** Both images were downscaled to 600 px wide (typica 600×356, carbonaria 600×291) from Wikimedia's 960 px thumbnails. Nothing else was changed. Under the license's ShareAlike terms, these resized copies stay under CC BY-SA 3.0.
