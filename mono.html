Natural Selection: Camouflage (Monochrome)

Each generation, a predator bird eats the 8 most visible moths (red finch), 8 more die at random (black skull stamp), and the 16 survivors pair up. Genes are 32-bit numbers (00000000–FFFFFFFF) shown as gray, drawn as moths. Each child gets the high 16 bits of one parent and the low 16 bits of the other.
