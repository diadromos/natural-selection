Natural Selection: Camouflage (Monochrome)

Color versionSpots versionSquirts version

Each generation, a predator eats the 8 most visible organisms (fangs), 8 more die at random (skull and crossbones), and the 16 survivors pair up. Genes are 32-bit numbers (00000000–FFFFFFFF) shown as gray. Each child gets the high 16 bits of one parent and the low 16 bits of the other.
